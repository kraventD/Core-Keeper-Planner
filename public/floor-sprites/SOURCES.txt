Core Keeper Planner floor sprite catalog

The PNGs in this folder are 16×16 inventory icon images for Core Keeper floor,
rug, floor-tile, and ground-block items. They are used as tile textures in the
planner. The item names and icon files were cross-referenced against the Core
Keeper wiki and retrieved from the public item-icon directory at
https://corekeeper.wiki/assets/icons/items/ (for example, WoodFloor.png).

Wiki reference pages:
https://corekeeper.atma.gg/en/Category:Floor
https://corekeeper.atma.gg/en/Environment
https://corekeeper.atma.gg/en/Rug

Core Keeper is the property of Pugstorm and its respective rights holders.
These local files are included for development and testing in this planner.
